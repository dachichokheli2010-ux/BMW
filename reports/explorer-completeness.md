# Explorer completeness, missing images and review queue

The retained 93-car catalogue, its engine index and the supplied parts registry. This audit measures gaps; placeholders never count as complete content.

Checked 2026-10-20. **The zero-gap definition of done is not met.** Structural integrity checks and complete researched content are different checks. The checker does not treat a placeholder, an unavailable label, or a factory homepage link as a completed fact.

- 93 retained car profiles and 30 engine/drive-family entries.
- 182 parts indexed by this audit run.
- 2222 missing car fields; 70 missing engine fields; 364 missing part fields.
- 5872 field-level research/review tasks.
- 766 missing required image slots across all indexed types.

## Evidence policy

Two distinct source documents are required for cross-checked fields. Two BMW documents are two references, not independent publishers. Null means not verified. User-visible unavailable labels do not make a factual field complete. Application-specific power and displacement are not pooled into a family specification. Current model discovery uses BMW Germany and selected BMW Group releases. It is not a complete worldwide variant inventory.

A car’s existing verified field may have only one checked document. It remains a review task under the requested two-source rule. Engine output tables preserve hp/PS, market, vehicle-system hybrid output and individual model context. Engine-family production dates are not inferred from represented vehicle years.

## Machine-readable handoff

- `explorer-needs-review.json`: every missing field, source-linked claim and verified claim that still needs a second document.
- `explorer-missing-images.json`: every missing required image with a suggested Commons search.
- `current-lineup-coverage.md`: current-model discovery and historical omissions.
- `explorer-completeness.json`: all of the above in one report.

Run `node scripts/check-completeness.mjs --write` to regenerate. The default command fails on invalid structure or media references; `--strict` also fails while content or image gaps remain. No new or unlicensed media is downloaded by the checker.
