# Model completeness and photo audit

Current catalogue after Phase 1 source and photo safeguards; unavailable is explicit and does not mean researched content is complete.

The catalogue has **93 models**, **99 verified fields**, **843 source-linked fields awaiting datum-level review**, and **2378 unavailable fields**. 493 fields do not apply to the selected powertrain. **0 profiles meet every requested verified-content and required-photo slot**.

Available safe media: 91/93 models have an exterior reference; 6/93 have a positively matched engine photograph. 160 unique local WebP photographs are used, with intrinsic dimensions and recorded author/license/source. Dashboard and seat slots require identified views; a generic interior label does not establish both. Wheel/detail photos are optional.

## Accuracy and provenance

A source year is the identified specification year, not an inferred production range or publication date. Null means unknown.

Existing umbrella factory links are labelled source-linked, not independently verified. Verified fields retain the exact model, market, unit and specification context. G80's source publishes 0–60 mph and mph top speeds; 0–100 km/h remains unavailable. US, EU and DIN weight conventions remain distinct.

Manufacturer images with all-rights-reserved metadata are excluded from public model media pending applicable permission. [BMW PressClub terms](https://www.press.bmwgroup.com/global/info/legal) restrict material to press use and prohibit commercial reuse; generic PressClub terms do not establish rights to BMW M or Classic website downloads. Two legacy Commons credits labelled only “Attribution” also remain excluded under the requested license whitelist.

The previous EV-variant clone copied drivetrain, boost, battery and model-year facts from the photo donor. It now shares photography only, so absent variant-specific facts remain unavailable. M54B25 images are excluded for M54B30 cars; the later F90 S63 image is excluded for F10 M5. Engine-family extras are not substituted for a positively matched model application.
